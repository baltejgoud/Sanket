## Intent

A silent, seamlessly looping motion-graphics piece that replaces the static
image on the right of the SANKET website hero. Buyers (retailers, brands,
distributors) should grasp the product in one glance: outside signals flow in,
SANKET draws a forecast with a range, recommends an exact order, and stockouts
fall. Calm, premium, product-UI feel; no voice, no music.

## Customizations

- Seamless loop: last frame matches the first.
- Brand: background #FAF9F6, cobalt #2563EB, ink #0F172A, green #059669, red #DC2626.
- Fonts: Manrope (display) + DM Sans (UI), matching the website.

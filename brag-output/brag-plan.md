# SANKET / PRISMA — A clearer future

SANKET is a forecasting and autonomous supply chain decision platform. This project is an interactive marketing sandbox; its examples are illustrative, not production telemetry.

## Angle and hook
A global inventory problem becomes a precise, actionable decision. Begin with the brief's $1.75 trillion planning figure, then show the actual forecasting console. High-key studio lighting, warm alabaster, cobalt precision, crisp editorial hierarchy. Tone: cinematic / polished. No narration. Landscape 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, exactly 25 seconds.

## User flow
Inspect demand → fuse a viral trend signal → obtain a probabilistic forecast → approve a replenishment suggestion → inspect financial impact and tenant security.

## Source material
src/App.tsx, src/data.ts, src/styles.css and public/assets/supply-chain.png. Use a screenshot of the real command console, with fictional SKU data. No contact or customer details appear in the film.

## Identity
Background #FAF9F6 / #FFFFFF. Text #0F172A. Accent #2563EB. Emerald #059669. Display and body: locally bundled Manrope. The connected alabaster supply network is the signature visual.

## Storyboard
1. 0–5 seconds: Macro problem. $1.75 trillion, inventory distortion, global supply network. Large stable statistic with quick warehouse/node labels. Caption identifies the figure as a planning assumption.
2. 5–12 seconds: Forecasting intelligence. 11 core models, zero-shot Chronos, 12 external signals. The actual console is the centerpiece; FRED, Google Trends, Weather, and TikTok feed into it. Headline settles early and remains readable.
3. 12–19 seconds: Autonomous decisions. SLSQP quantile stacking minimizes pinball loss. Replenishment 1,240 units, 10–25% inventory reduction target, 20–40% stockout reduction target. Separate the targets from guarantees.
4. 19–25 seconds: Enterprise impact and security. $1.12M annualized scenario impact, under-30-day scenario payback. PostgreSQL RLS, GxP / Part 11 architecture. Final SANKET brand lock-up and A clearer future for your supply chain.

## Audio

## Planning rubric
Product: enterprise supply chain intelligence. Audience: operations and finance leaders. Value: turn uncertain demand into defensible decisions. Differentiator: foundation forecasts plus external signals plus optimization. Signature: quantile fan chart. Action: sandbox walkthrough. Tone: precise and confident. Story: uncertainty to action. Flow: scenario → forecast → decision → ROI.
